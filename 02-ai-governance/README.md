# 02 · AI governance

Jasper sells governance — a Governance product page, an AI policy template for
customers, "built-in governance" in the MCP server's marketing. The posting is candid
that internally the work is to "drive, improve, and help implement." The distance
between governance sold and governance operated is this pillar.

- [Article 50 readiness](art50-readiness/) — the 30-day deliverable, scoped: surface
  inventory seeded from public product pages, provider-vs-deployer duty split, marking
  plan ahead of the 2 December 2026 date.
- [Model change management](../03-secure-development/model-change-management.md) — the
  Jasper-specific control surface: routing changes as a governed change type.
- The frameworks arrive by render, not by project: the engine ships ISO 42001, NIST AI
  RMF, and EU AI Act profiles over the same control set ([engine bridge](../generated/engine-bridge.md)).

The one hard rule carries over from the engine unchanged: **AI drafts, computes, and
blocks; it never signs.** Pass/fail is deterministic code; evidence is computed from
systems of record; a human approves everything that becomes record.
