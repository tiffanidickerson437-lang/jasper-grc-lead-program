# The regulatory clock

Every date that governs the plan. All checked against the named sources on
31 August 2026. The same clock exists as data in
[`regulatory-dates.yaml`](../04-evidence-and-audit/data/regulatory-dates.yaml), where
[`audit_clock.py`](../04-evidence-and-audit/data/audit_clock.py) validates it and
computes the day counts — this page is the prose; the arithmetic is code.

## What already happened

| Date | Event | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-01 | Texas TRAIGA takes effect | In force in Jasper's founding state. Marketing-content AI is mostly outside "consequential decision" scope, but questionnaires increasingly ask anyway |
| 2026-05-07 | EU digital-omnibus grandfathering | Moved **high-risk** AI Act deadlines to Dec 2027 / Aug 2028. **Did not move Article 50** — the misreading most stale timelines are built on |
| 2026-08-02 | EU AI Act Art. 50(1)/(3)/(4) apply | AI-interaction and synthetic-content disclosure duties, live now. Penalties under Art. 99 to €15M / 3% of worldwide turnover |
| 2026-08-14 | Jasper ISO/IEC 27001:2022 announced | Starts the surveillance clock. Two weeks later the certification appeared on no marketing surface — finding F1 |
| 2026-08-14 | Trust center migrates SafeBase → Vanta | All prior customer access reset; mixed document vintages on the new portal — finding F2's territory |

## The sentence worth keeping loaded

The omnibus moved the high-risk timeline and left Article 50 alone. A candidate — or a
vendor, or a slide — citing "the AI Act lands next year" is reading the headline, not
the article. Precision on live law is the cheapest credibility available to a GRC seat,
and the easiest to lose.
