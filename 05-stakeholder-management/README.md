# 05 · Stakeholder management

The posting's centre of gravity: questionnaires, RFPs, audits, and public claims that
must agree with each other. Trust review throughput is revenue velocity here — Jasper
sells into financial services and healthcare marketing teams, and every deal passes
through a security review.

- [Public claims consistency](public-claims-consistency/) — findings F1 and F3,
  machine-checked against observations of Jasper's own surfaces, with the rendered
  findings report guarded by CI.

The claim this pillar makes is small and sharp: **claims hygiene is a revenue
control.** An enterprise buyer lands on whichever page ranks; if that page asserts what
the trust center cannot support, the security review opens on a contradiction — and the
questionnaire turnaround KPI pays for it.
